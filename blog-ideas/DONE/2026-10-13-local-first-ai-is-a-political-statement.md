## Local-First AI Is a Political Statement Now

When I started building tools that run entirely on your own hardware, with your own models, no API calls to third parties — I thought I was making a technical choice. It turns out I was making a values statement.

This is a post about why local-first AI stopped being a technical preference and started being a position you have to defend. And what that means for anyone building in this space.
