## The Human in the Loop Is Not a Safety Feature

Every AI safety framework includes "human in the loop" as a core principle. I used to agree with this. Now I think it's a way of deferring the actual hard question: what happens when the human isn't paying attention, isn't qualified to judge, or just trusts the AI?

The human in the loop sounds like safety. It often isn't.
