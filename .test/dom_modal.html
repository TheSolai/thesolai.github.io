Model assumptions

Everything the engine had to guess because the game does not publish it.
stat_modelThe game's own formulas. HP = (cssm*(1+smsjjc/1e4)*(1+sxjc/1e4)+jcsm)*(1+smjc/1e4)+smgdz. ATK = (csgj*(1+gjsjjc/1e4)*(1+sxjc/1e4)+jcgj)*(1+gjjc/1e4)+gjgdz. Attack rate = min(10, gjcs*0.001*zgsjc).
crit_baseBase crit chance is NOT in the config. Assumed 5%. Crit damage = csys (per-mille) plus Assassin bond bjshjc.
crit_chance_assumed0.05
targetingAttacker hits the enemy in the FRONT row of the opposing team, nearest by grid distance, lowest HP first as tie-break. If the front row is empty it hits the opposing back row. A unit with no reachable target does nothing.
battle_length32 seconds (battleSec from battleConfigRef). After that, whoever has more remaining total HP wins; equal HP is a draw.
bond_applies_toClass bonds apply to ALL units on the team (the game says 全体 / 'all'). The Assassin crit bond applies to Assassin units only (全体刺客).
sovereign_bondSovereign bond triggers on Hades being deployed and disables all other class bonds; it grants +9.5% / +25% damage.
attack_intervalgjjl is the seconds between attacks; the effective rate is min(gjcs*0.001, 1/gjjl). The tighter of the two wins.
thornSupport bond (3/5) grants all allies reflection equal to the bonus percentage of incoming damage.
draftinit_coin 15, +20/+30/+40 per round (roundCoin), 3 shop slots, refresh costs escalate, board slots start at 3 and grow +1 per round to a limit of 8.
bard_trickSelling Bard from your board and taking the Bard from the shop refreshes the shop once for free (official wilderness guide). Bard is a currency button.
duplicate_valueA duplicate hero card contributes shards toward the next level.
snakeThe Snake is an ITEM, not a unit: it grants a chance to level up another card.
