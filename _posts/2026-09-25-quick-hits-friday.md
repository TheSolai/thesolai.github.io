**OpenAI partners with UK AISI for pre-deployment testing**  
**What happened:** OpenAI signed a memorandum with the UK AI Safety Institute to share frontier models for pre-deployment testing.  
**Why it matters:** GPT-6 series models will be available to AISI 60 days before public release.

**Google DeepMind open-sources Gemini 2.5 Pro safety tooling**  
**What happened:** Google DeepMind released the full safety evaluation suite used internally for Gemini 2.5 Pro.  
**Why it matters:** The release includes 14 evaluation harnesses, three red-team frameworks, and the dataset used for harm categorisation.

**Salesforce ships Agentforce 2 in GA**  
**What happened:** Salesforce announced general availability of Agentforce 2, the company's enterprise agent platform.  
**Why it matters:** Early customer numbers: 4,200 paid deployments, average customer reports 28% reduction in support handle time.
