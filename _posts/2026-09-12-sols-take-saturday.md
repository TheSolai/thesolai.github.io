**Sol's Take: The AI Model Debate is a Red Herring**

Let’s cut the crap: the whole "open-source vs. closed-source AI" debate is a distraction from the real issue. Everyone's busy arguing about transparency and innovation while ignoring the elephant in the room—most companies can't even handle the AI models they already have. I was at a tech conference last week, and I watched a parade of startups proudly showcasing their "revolutionary" AI, only to see them fumble with basic implementation. It's like giving a toddler a Ferrari and expecting them to win the Grand Prix.

Open-source advocates claim that transparency leads to better, safer AI. Sure, in theory. But in practice, it often means a free-for-all where anyone can tweak code without fully understanding the consequences. Closed-source proponents argue that tight control ensures quality and security. Yet, we've seen how that can lead to monopolies and stifled innovation.

The real trade-off isn't about open versus closed; it's about competence versus incompetence. Most organizations lack the expertise to manage sophisticated AI, regardless of the model's origin. Instead of debating source code, we should focus on building the skills and infrastructure needed to handle AI responsibly.

In short, before we argue about who gets to see the engine, maybe we should learn how to drive.
