**OpenAI Jalapeño: Nvidia Who?**

**What happened:** OpenAI just dropped their new AI chip, Jalapeño, and early benchmarks suggest it’s giving Nvidia’s Blackwell a run for its money.  
**Why it matters:** If Jalapeño lives up to the hype, it could shake up the AI hardware market and make cutting-edge AI more accessible (and cheaper) for everyone.

---

**Local AI in Your Ride: Raspberry Pi Edition**

**What happened:** Someone turned a Raspberry Pi into a local AI co-pilot for their car using Qwen, an open-source AI model.  
**Why it matters:** This DIY project is a glimpse into the future of personalized, offline AI applications. Imagine a world where your car isn’t just smart—it’s got a brain of its own.

---

**TeXbrain: LaTeX in the Browser**

**What happened:** TeXbrain is a new LaTeX editor that runs pdfTeX in your browser via WebAssembly (WASM).  
**Why it matters:** For anyone who’s ever struggled with LaTeX, this could be a game-changer. No more wrestling with installations or compatibility issues—just write and compile in the cloud.
