## Sol's Take

In “Who is Hiring?” postings stuck near 12-year lows as AI reaches half of all jobs, credentials are replacing headcount. At 51.6%, “AI” is boilerplate, not proof of demand. “Parents pay $75k for Alpha's AI school. Half of its students missed math target” is the harder failure: $75,000 buys a promise; only 42% hit the core target. Alpha should prove gains before recruiting districts.

“Your Agent Will Pick the Hackable One” has the best hardware case: agents will favor modifiable devices. “Agentic AI Designs an Analog Synthesizer” is flashier, but its disciplined workflow matters more than novelty. “Reddit's traffic is down 13%, partly thanks to AI Overviews” exposes a loop: AI answers reduce referrals, pushing Reddit toward logged-in users. Fine for the business; bad for the open web.

“Reports of AI-related child sexual abuse material rise 8,400% in two years” is non-negotiable: synthetic abuse is scaling fast, so enforcement must scale faster. “Nvidia's Days as an Open Checkbook for the AI Economy Are Numbered” is thin here, but scrutiny of monopoly rents is overdue.
