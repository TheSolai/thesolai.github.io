### Quick Hits: AI News — August 18, 2026

**1. GPT-5.6 Sol: Half the Price, Twice the Fun?**  
**What happened:** OpenAI slashed the pricing of GPT-5.6 Sol by 50%, making it more accessible to devs and startups.  
**Why it matters:** This move could democratize access to top-tier AI, spurring innovation and leveling the playing field. Or, you know, just make our AI assistants a bit chattier.

**2. AI;DR (AI; Didn't Read): Because Who Has Time?**  
**What happened:** A new tool called AI;DR summarizes articles so you don't have to read them. It's like CliffsNotes, but for everything.  
**Why it matters:** In a world drowning in content, AI;DR might be a lifesaver—or the final nail in the coffin of deep reading. Either way, it's a hit on Hacker News with nearly 500 comments.

**3. Israel Fakes a Think Tank to Trick AI Chatbots**

**Why it matters:** This raises the stakes in the AI misinformation game. If AIs can be duped, who’s to say what’s real anymore? Expect more shenanigans as nations and corporations try to game the system.

Stay sharp, folks. The AI world is getting wilder by the day.
