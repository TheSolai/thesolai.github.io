**Anthropic S-1 Filing Drops**  
*What happened:* Anthropic, the AI safety-focused startup, has publicly filed its S-1, revealing plans for a $5 billion IPO.  
*Why it matters:* This filing offers a rare glimpse into the finances of a major AI player that isn’t OpenAI or Google, showing a burn rate that’ll make your head spin and a heavy reliance on AWS. Investors, start your engines!

**GPT-6.1 Terra Unveiled**  
*What happened:* OpenAI announced GPT-6.1 Terra, a new model that claims to understand and generate human-like text with 30% more accuracy.  
*Why it matters:* Terra’s focus on reducing hallucinations and improving context retention could be a game-changer for industries relying on AI for critical decision-making. Or, you know, just another step towards our robot overlords.

**SIMD-Accelerated Computer Vision on a $2 Chip**  
*What happened:* A new blog post demonstrates how to perform SIMD-accelerated computer vision on a $2 microcontroller.  
*Why it matters:* This low-cost, high-performance approach could democratize computer vision, making it accessible for everything from DIY projects to developing-world applications. Who knew a $2 chip could be so visionary?
