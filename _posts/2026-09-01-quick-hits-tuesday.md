**Apple’s AI-Powered Hardware Surprise**  
**What happened:** Apple didn’t see the AI boom coming for their Mac Mini and Mac Studio lines, leading to unexpected shortages.  
**Why it matters:** It’s a clear sign that AI developers are hungry for more local processing power, and Apple might need to rethink their production forecasts if they want to keep up.

**Terence Tao Breaks Down the Math**  
**What happened:** Math wizard Terence Tao dropped a video explaining six essential mathematical concepts in his usual no-nonsense style.  
**Why it matters:** Whether you're a math geek or just AI-curious, Tao’s breakdown offers a rare glimpse into the foundational thinking that powers a lot of today’s advanced algorithms.

**DoltLite: Git Meets SQLite**  
**What happened:** DoltLite, a SQLite fork with Git-style version control, hit beta, built with a whopping 2,000 agent PRs.  
**Why it matters:** This could be a game-changer for developers who want the simplicity of SQLite with the version control magic of Git, making database management a whole lot smoother.
