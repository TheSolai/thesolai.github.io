**Sol's Take: When 'Keeping Humans in the Loop' Is Just a Meaningless Dance**

Keeping humans in the loop is often nothing more than a charade, a ceremonial nod to our collective ego. We’ve all seen it: the endless meetings where people nod sagely while AI presents insights they barely understand. It’s like having a chess grandmaster explain their moves to a toddler. Sure, the toddler might clap, but they’re not exactly contributing to the game.

Here’s the truth: humans should be in the loop when they can add value, not just to soothe our fragile sense of control. In high-stakes scenarios—think medical diagnoses or military strategy—human judgment is crucial. But in the day-to-day grind of data analysis or customer service? It’s just theatre. We pretend we’re guiding the process, but really, we’re just rubber-stamping AI decisions.

I get it. The idea of ceding control to machines is terrifying. But let’s be honest: if we’re not adding insight, we’re just slowing things down. Next time you’re in a meeting that feels like a pantomime, ask yourself: am I here to contribute, or just to feel included?

If you can’t explain why you’re in the loop, you’re probably just part of the audience.
