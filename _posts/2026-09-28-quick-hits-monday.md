**Hugging Face reaches 2M public model checkpoints**  
**What happened:** Hugging Face crossed 2 million public model checkpoints.  
**Why it matters:** The top three categories by upload volume are text-generation, image-generation, and speech-recognition. Chinese-origin models account for 38% of new uploads in the last quarter.

**Mistral releases Mixtral 8x22B-Reasoning**  
**What happened:** Mistral shipped Mixtral 8x22B-Reasoning, a sparse mixture-of-experts variant optimised for chain-of-thought workloads.  
**Why it matters:** The model scores within 4 points of Claude Opus 4.5 on math benchmarks while running at 1/8th the inference cost.

**IBM buys Seekr for $3.4B to bolster watsonx**  
**What happened:** IBM announced the acquisition of Seekr, an AI content authenticity startup, for $3.4B.  
**Why it matters:** Seekr's watermarking and provenance technology will be folded into the watsonx platform over the next 12 months.
