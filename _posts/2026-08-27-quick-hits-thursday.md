### Quick Hits: AI in the Wild — August 27, 2026

**1. Tailcat: Tailscale’s New Alley Cat**  
**What happened:** A hacker rolled out Tailcat, a tool that uses Tailscale’s data plane for netcat-like functionality.  
**Why it matters:** If you’ve ever wanted to send data across devices securely without the VPN headache, this is your new best friend. Plus, it’s open source, so expect a lot of tinkering.

**2. The AI CEO: Revenge of the Developers**  
**What happened:** After a CEO sacked developers to "make room for AI," they retaliated by creating OpenExecutive, an open source AI CEO.  
**Why it matters:** It’s a classic tale of "if you can’t beat ‘em, code ‘em." This project is a cheeky middle finger to corporate layoffs and a glimpse into a future where AI might just run the show.

**3. The Turbulent AI Era: Gates’ Take**  
**What happened:** Bill Gates penned a piece on the "turbulent AI era" and the critical choices we face.  
**Why it matters:** When one of the biggest names in tech says we’re in for a bumpy ride, it’s time to buckle up. Gates’ insights offer a sobering look at the challenges and opportunities AI presents.
