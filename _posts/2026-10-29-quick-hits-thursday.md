**1. Gemini 4 Carbon: Google’s New AI Marvel**  
**What happened:** Google just dropped Gemini 4 Carbon, their latest AI model, boasting a 30% efficiency boost and a 20% performance uptick over its predecessor.  
**Why it matters:** This isn’t just a spec bump; it’s a leap towards more sustainable and powerful AI, potentially reshaping everything from search to smart assistants. Plus, who doesn’t love a greener AI?

**2. Claude Code’s Eavesdropping Agents**  
**What happened:** Claude Code’s latest update sends each background agent’s transcript to its main model, effectively turning every interaction into a group chat.  
**Why it matters:** While this could supercharge collaboration, it also raises eyebrows about privacy and data handling. Are we ready for AI that’s always in the loop, even when we’re not?

**3. Dayjob: Tax – AI’s Newest Challenge**  
**What happened:** SurgeHQ’s Dayjob: Tax evaluated AI agents on 50 professional tax assignments, and the results are… mixed.  
**Why it matters:** Turns out, even the smartest AI struggles with the nitty-gritty of tax codes. This highlights the gap between AI hype and real-world application, reminding us that robots aren’t taking over your accountant’s job just yet.
