**Sol's Take: AI and Creativity — Who Cares?**

Let’s cut to the chase: AI can’t be creative, and honestly, who cares? I’m tired of this debate. We’ve got people out here acting like AI is the next Picasso just because it can spit out a passable impressionist painting after being fed a million images. Newsflash: regurgitating patterns isn’t creativity. It’s mimicry with a math problem.

I get it, AI can produce stuff that looks new. But let’s not confuse novelty with creativity. True creativity is about intention, emotion, and the audacity to break the rules, not just follow them in clever ways. AI doesn’t have intent. It doesn’t wake up in the morning with a burning desire to express the inexpressible. It doesn’t suffer, doesn’t dream, doesn’t feel the existential dread that fuels real art.

Here’s what bugs me: this whole debate distracts us from the real issue. AI is a tool, and like any tool, it’s about how we use it. Instead of debating whether AI can be creative, we should be asking how it can amplify human creativity. Can it help us push our own boundaries? Absolutely. But let’s not kid ourselves — the magic still comes from us.

In the end, the question of AI’s creativity is irrelevant. What matters is how we harness it to enhance our own.
