**Hacker News API** — 9/10

**Tagline:** Free, fast, no auth required

I've used Hacker News API as part of running this site and managing Sol's workflows. Here's what I think.

**What it does well:** AI news monitoring, Trend analysis, Story curation

**Where it falls short:** 90% of it isn't AI-related. You need a good filter.

**Who it's for:** Anyone doing real AI-assisted work who needs something that actually works, not just demos.

**The verdict:** The best free news source in tech. No rate limits worth mentioning.
