Developed by a team of AI enthusiasts and designers in the UK, Pele takes Mermaid diagrams and transforms them into customizable, scalable vector graphics (SVGs). This means you can tweak colors, fonts, and layouts to your heart’s content, all while maintaining the clarity and precision of your original diagram.
