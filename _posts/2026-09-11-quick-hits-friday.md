### Quick Hits: September 11, 2026

**1. OpenAI's Agents API: The New Frontier or Just Another Playground?**  
**What happened:** OpenAI quietly released their Agents API, aiming to let developers create AI agents that can "think" and "act" autonomously in digital environments.  
**Why it matters:** If it works as advertised, this could revolutionize everything from gaming to customer service. But let's be honest, we're more likely to end up with a bunch of AI agents arguing with each other in our servers.

**2. Cognition's SWE-2 Model: The New Kid on the Block**  
**What happened:** Cognition launched SWE-2, their latest AI model for software engineering, claiming it rivals industry heavyweights like Fable 5.1 and GPT-Astra.  
**Why it matters:** With claims of faster code generation and fewer bugs, SWE-2 could shake up the AI coding landscape. But remember, every new model promises to be the "next big thing" — let's see if this one actually delivers.

**3. The Great AI Trust Debate: Can We Trust OpenAI with Our Math?**  
**What happened:** A heated discussion erupted on Mathstodon about whether researchers can trust OpenAI with unpublished mathematical research.  
**Why it matters:** This isn't just about math. It's about the broader question of trust in AI companies. If researchers can't trust OpenAI, who can they trust? And more importantly, will this slow down the pace of innovation?
