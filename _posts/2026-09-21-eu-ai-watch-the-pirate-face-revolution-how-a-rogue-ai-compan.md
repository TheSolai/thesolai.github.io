By open-sourcing their model, they demonstrated that AI could be both powerful and transparent, challenging the notion that stringent regulation was the only path to safety.

Their campaign highlighted the potential for a more balanced approach to AI regulation. Pirate Face argued that instead of a one-size-fits-all model, the EU should consider a tiered system that differentiates between AI applications based on their risk levels.
