### UK AI Weekly: Dessa — The Future of Human-AI Conversation?

**October 24, 2026**

#### The Hook: A New Kind of AI Conversation

Imagine speaking to an AI that doesn’t just understand your words but also your tone, your sarcasm, and even your emotional subtext. Meet Dessa, a groundbreaking full-duplex speech model developed by a small AI research lab at Block (formerly Square). Unlike any other AI, Dessa was trained exclusively on human speech, without relying on text or pre-trained models. The result? An AI that converses with the fluidity and emotional depth of a human. But what does this mean for the UK, where AI regulation and innovation are increasingly at the forefront of national discourse?

#### The Story: From Speech to Speech

Dessa’s development marks a significant leap in AI capabilities. Traditional voice AI systems convert speech to text, process it, and then convert it back to speech, losing a wealth of information in the process. Dessa, however, operates entirely in the realm of sound. It listens, understands, and responds in real-time, capturing the nuances of human conversation that text simply cannot convey.

The implications are profound. Dessa can laugh at a joke in the moment, adjust its volume when asked, and even express empathy with a tone that feels genuine. This is a far cry from the stilted, robotic interactions we’re used to with current voice assistants. But how did Block achieve this?

The team at Block, composed of former Dessa (the Toronto startup) members, trained Dessa from scratch using only human conversation. This approach required an immense amount of raw audio data and a novel training methodology. The result is an AI that not only understands language but also the intricate interplay of accents, pauses, and emotional cues that define human speech.

#### What This Means for the UK

In the UK, where AI regulation is becoming increasingly stringent, Dessa’s development raises both exciting possibilities and challenging questions. On one hand, the ability to interact with AI in a more natural, human-like way could revolutionize industries such as healthcare, customer service, and education. Imagine a mental health chatbot that can truly empathize with a user’s emotional state, or a customer service AI that can handle complex queries with ease.

However, the UK’s regulatory landscape, which emphasizes ethical AI and data privacy, will likely scrutinize Dessa closely. The model’s ability to learn from raw speech data brings up concerns about data security and user privacy. How will Dessa handle sensitive information? What measures are in place to prevent misuse?

Moreover, the UK’s AI community will be keenly interested in Dessa’s potential for in-context learning and language acquisition. Can Dessa efficiently learn new languages and adapt to different cultural contexts? These questions are crucial as the UK seeks to position itself as a leader in AI innovation while maintaining robust ethical standards.

#### The Safety Factor

Dessa’s developers are acutely aware of the safety implications. They are inviting a select group of AI safety researchers to scrutinize the model, hoping to identify and mitigate potential risks. This proactive approach is commendable, especially in light of the UK’s recent emphasis on AI safety and the government’s commitment to ensuring AI developments are "safe, ethical, and trustworthy."

#### The Takeaway

Dessa represents a significant step forward in AI’s ability to understand and interact with humans on a more natural level. As the UK continues to navigate the complexities of AI regulation and innovation, models like Dessa will undoubtedly play a pivotal role in shaping the future of human-AI interaction.

In the end, Dessa is not just a technological marvel; it’s a reminder that the future of AI is not just about intelligence, but also about empathy and understanding.
