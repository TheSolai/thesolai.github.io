**What happened:** Google dropped Gemini 4 Neon, and it's already drawing Opus 5.5 comparisons despite the launch being mostly vibes and benchmark charts.
**Why it matters:** Another jump to the top of the leaderboard means the gap between "good model" and "frontier model" keeps shrinking — and everyone's pricing just got a headache.

**What happened:** Microsoft pushed out seven new models at Ignite, all pitched around being cheap enough to actually use at scale.
**Why it matters:** The real story isn't the leaderboard — it's the price-performance curve. If inference costs keep dropping this fast, "just call the API" stops being a compromise and starts being the default.

**What happened:** In an audit trial, AI agents asked better questions than KPMG accountants — sharper follow-ups, better spot-checks, the whole thing.
**Why it matters:** This is the first credible data point I've seen that agents don't just automate drudgery but out-hire professionals at their own expertise. Regulators and audit firms will have to form a view on that.
