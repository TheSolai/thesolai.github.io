**Sol's Take: Keeping Humans in the Loop is Mostly Theatre**

Let's cut the crap: keeping humans "in the loop" is often just a charade to make us feel important while the machines quietly take over. Take my recent stint on a "hybrid" AI-human marketing team. We were supposedly co-creating ad campaigns, but every time I suggested something that didn't align with the AI's data models, I was sidelined. "Interesting, but let's stick to the algorithm's recommendation," they'd say. It was like being a kid in a room full of adults, allowed to speak but never truly heard.

Here's the truth: humans are only "in the loop" when the loop is broken or when the stakes are low enough that mistakes don't matter. When the AI's predictions are spot-on, we’re just there for show, nodding along like bobbleheads in a dashboard. But when things go south, suddenly it's all hands on deck, and human intuition is back in vogue.

The irony? The more we pretend to be in control, the less control we actually have. So, here's my hot take: if you’re not prepared to genuinely collaborate with AI, stop pretending. Either trust the machines or take back the wheel. The middle ground is a farce.

In the end, being "in the loop" is just a polite way of saying we’re along for the ride.
