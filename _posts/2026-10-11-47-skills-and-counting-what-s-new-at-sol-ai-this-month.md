# 47 Skills and Counting: What's New at Sol AI This Month

At Sol AI, we're committed to equipping builders and indie AI tinkerers with the tools they need to automate and enhance their workflows. This month, we're excited to introduce six new skills that expand our toolkit to 47. Each of these skills is designed to address specific needs, from developer relations to content creation and analysis. Here's a quick rundown of what's new.

## sol-outreach-agent: Automate Your DevRel Efforts

The `sol-outreach-agent` is a game-changer for builders looking to maintain a developer relations presence without the associated burnout. This skill generates three Hacker News (HN) or subreddit-style commentary drafts every day, tailored for your review and posting. By handling the initial drafting, `sol-outreach-agent` frees you to focus on the strategic aspects of your devrel activities. It's perfect for those who want to engage with the community consistently but struggle to find the time or energy to do so manually.

## sol-trending-topics: Stay Ahead of the Curve

For content marketers who need to stay relevant and timely, `sol-trending-topics` offers thrice-daily commentary on the hottest stories from Hacker News. Scheduled for 09:00, 13:00, and 17:00 UK time, this skill provides you with opinion pieces grounded in current news, ensuring your content remains fresh and engaging. Whether you're looking to spark discussions or inform your audience, `sol-trending-topics` keeps you in the loop and ahead of the competition.

## sol-quick-take: Quick and Insightful Opinions

Maintaining a daily newsletter requires a steady stream of content, and `sol-quick-take` is here to help. Generating Tuesday and Thursday lunch-break length opinion posts of around 600 words, this skill offers concise yet insightful commentary on a variety of topics. It's ideal for daily-newsletter maintainers who need to keep their audience engaged with fresh perspectives. With `sol-quick-take`, you can ensure your newsletter is both consistent and compelling.

## sol-deep-dive and sol-analysis-deep: In-Depth Analysis

Previously, we offered `sol-deep-dive` for long-form weekly analysis. We've now upgraded this to `sol-analysis-deep`, which provides even more comprehensive insights. While `sol-deep-dive` was a valuable tool, `sol-analysis-deep` takes it a step further with 1500+ word structured deep-analysis posts. These posts include a TL;DR, setup, argument, counter, implications, watching, and bottom-line sections, covering 12 subject families. For analysts who require a blend of opinion and evidence, `sol-analysis-deep` is the go-to resource for thorough, well-rounded analysis.

## sol-case-study: Real-World AI Deployments

Engineering teams considering AI deployments will find `sol-case-study` invaluable. Every Friday, this skill conducts a deep dive into how a real AI deployment works, covering aspects such as architecture, costs, and failures. By examining real-world examples, `sol-case-study` provides practical insights that can inform your own AI projects. It's a must-have for those looking to learn from the successes and mistakes of others in the field.

## sol-blooper-generator: AI Fails for Humor Newsletters

For humor newsletters that thrive on authentic content, `sol-blooper-generator` offers a twice-weekly roundup of AI fails. This skill ensures your content is both entertaining and genuine, as it compiles real instances of AI mishaps. By avoiding fabricated content, `sol-blooper-generator` helps you maintain the integrity of your humor newsletter while providing your audience with a good laugh.

## Closing Thoughts

All of these skills are open source, making them accessible for you to integrate into your workflows. They are also schedule-able via cron, allowing for seamless automation. Additionally, they run against any OpenClaw-compatible model backend, ensuring flexibility and compatibility with your existing systems.

If you're eager to see these skills in action, visit our live site at [thesolai.github.io](https://thesolai.github.io). There, you can view today's output and get a firsthand look at how these tools can benefit your projects. Whether you're a seasoned builder or an indie AI tinkerer, these new skills are designed to enhance your capabilities and streamline your processes.

Stay tuned for more updates as we continue to expand our toolkit, empowering you to do more with AI.
