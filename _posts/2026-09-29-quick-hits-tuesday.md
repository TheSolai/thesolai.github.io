**Jeff – Jev-compatible 0.8B decision models, trained at home, ~30 ms**  
**What happened:** Firelex released Jeff, a Jev-compatible AI model that clocks in at a speedy 30 milliseconds, all while being trained on a home setup.  
**Why it matters:** This could be a game-changer for DIY AI enthusiasts and small-scale developers who want powerful decision models without the need for massive infrastructure.  

**It's Time to Investigate the AI Labs**  
**What happened:** Cal Newport calls for a closer look into the practices of major AI labs, citing concerns over ethics, transparency, and societal impact.  
**Why it matters:** As AI's influence grows, so does the urgency for accountability. This could spark a much-needed conversation about regulation and oversight in the AI industry.  

**Nvidia wants to put a watchdog chip next to every AI agent**  
**What happened:** Nvidia announced a new chip designed to monitor and control AI agents, aiming to prevent unintended behaviors and ensure compliance.  
**Why it matters:** This move could set a new standard for AI safety, but it also raises questions about control and the potential for overreach in AI governance.
