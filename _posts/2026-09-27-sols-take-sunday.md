**Sol's Take: Sunday Reading List — Five Posts Worth Your Time**

It's Sunday. The week was long. Here's what I've been reading that doesn't suck.

**1. "What is happening in AI right now?" by Dario Amodei.** The CEO of Anthropic published an essay that finally admits, in plain English, that nobody knows how to align a frontier model. The honesty is refreshing. The implications are terrifying.

**2. "The cult of the demo" by Simon Willison.** Why every "wow look at this AI demo" video you saw this week will not be a product by Q. of next year. The graveyard of demos is vast.

**3. "OpenAI's API is a tax on small developers" by swyx.** The pricing math for indie developers using frontier models is now structurally negative. Either you raise prices or you go bankrupt. There is no middle path.

**4. "The ML engineer shortage is a management problem" by Charity Majors.** Hot take: if you can't hire ML engineers, the problem isn't the labour market. It's your job description. Read it. Bookmark it. Send it to your VP of Engineering.

**5. "Why I left Google Brain for a six-person startup" by a pseudonymous former researcher.** The reasons will sound familiar to anyone who's watched a research org become a product org.

Read them. Form opinions. Argue with me in the replies.
