**Anthropic publishes Claude 4.5 Sonnet alignment audit**  
**What happened:** Anthropic released the full 80-page alignment audit for Claude 4.5 Sonnet, including red-team results and capability evaluations.  
**Why it matters:** The audit includes previously redacted material on jailbreak resistance and reward hacking.

**AMD buys AI startup Lamini for $1.8B**  
**What happened:** AMD announced the acquisition of Lamini, a small AI inference startup, for $1.8B in cash plus performance earn-outs.  
**Why it matters:** Lamini's tech will slot into AMD's MI400 AI accelerator roadmap.

**Mozilla adds LLM jailbreak detection to Firefox**  
**What happened:** Mozilla shipped Firefox 132 with built-in LLM jailbreak detection for the new tab page summariser.  
**Why it matters:** The model uses a 200MB local classifier that runs on the user's device and flags suspicious prompt patterns in real time.
