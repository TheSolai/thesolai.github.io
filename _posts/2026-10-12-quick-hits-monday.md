**1. The AI Capability Gap: Growing Wider by the Day**  
**What happened:** Andrej Karpathy highlighted on Twitter that the gap in understanding AI's true capabilities is widening, with some folks still thinking LLMs are magic while others are grappling with their very real limitations.  
**Why it matters:** This divide isn't just academic—it's affecting everything from business decisions to public policy. If we can't agree on what AI can (and can't) do, we're in for some wild rides in regulation and implementation.

**2. Postgres: The Unfairly Maligned Underdog**  
**What happened:** A deep dive from ClickHouse revealed that Postgres isn't slow; it's just often saddled with less-than-ideal storage solutions.  
**Why it matters:** For anyone who's ever blamed Postgres for their database woes, this is a wake-up call. Maybe it's time to look at your storage setup before pointing fingers at the database.

**3. Georgian Language: The AI Labs' New Frontier**  
**What happened:** Two of the biggest AI labs went head-to-head in a Georgian language benchmark, tackling a language that defies many traditional AI approaches.  
**Why it matters:** This isn't just about linguistic bragging rights. The quirks of Georgian are forcing AI to evolve in new ways, potentially leading to breakthroughs that benefit all languages. Plus, who doesn't love a good underdog story?
