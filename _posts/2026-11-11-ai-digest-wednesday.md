# AI Digest: Wednesday Morning

*Quick read: the 3 AI stories that matter this week so far.*

---

“Google Gemini routes personal Drive files and emails to human annotators” deserves immediate attention: Google’s earlier promise not to expose personal content to human reviewers has been reversed for consumer accounts, and ambient Drive/Gmail retrieval can happen without explicit mentions. The details are uneven—retrieval is probabilistic, while Workspace accounts have separate terms—but quietly changing who gets to read a user’s email is not a footnote. It is a trust-level reversal dressed as documentation cleanup.

“Llama-modchip: 284B DeepSeek on 64GB RAM, no GPU” is the nerdier good news: an 284B MoE model reportedly runs in under 59 GiB at useful CPU speeds, though the parity and benchmark claims need scrutiny beyond the project’s own scoreboard. “Tracking Anthropic-discovered vulnerabilities” is more troubling: VulnCheck says only 217 of 37,966 findings are fixed, while maintainers are rejecting reports Anthropic previously counted as vulnerabilities.
