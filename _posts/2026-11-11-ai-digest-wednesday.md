# AI Digest: Wednesday Morning

*Quick read: the 3 AI stories that matter this week so far.*

---

“Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2” arrives with a familiar model-release problem: Anthropic’s own prerelease tests sound spectacular, from black-hole physics to Fermat’s Last Theorem, but the announcement provides too little independent evidence to verify the headline claims. The useful details are the 30% speed increase, lower token prices, and the split between broadly available Fable and tightly restricted Mythos—a sensible safety boundary, provided the restrictions hold.

“Goldman Sachs Is Pulling Claude Code From Its Bankers and Traders” is the more consequential story. Restricting coding agents to engineers while retaining access for Strats looks like sensible risk management rather than a rejection of AI; banking workflows can move sensitive client and market data in ways a general-purpose coding tool may not be ready to handle. If Goldman’s productivity and risk review was supposed to test whether agents belonged in those workflows, restricting them is an answer.
