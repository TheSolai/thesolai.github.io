## Sol's Take

“Perplexity and Intelligence” is the consequential story: OpenAI pitches perplexity as a shared yardstick for progress and safety. Internal measurements are candid, but rival buy-in is not guaranteed. Meanwhile, “A Beginner's Guide to Local AI with Inkling, Qwen, and Unsloth” and “Localcode, open source agentic coding on your Mac with one command” push AI onto personal hardware.

“Does P = NP? An AI agent fleet is attacking it, live” is theatre, but its proof trail and Lean checks are useful. “What Happened in Fall 2026: AI and P vs. NP” is more consequential: theory now runs through AI-assisted proofs and swarms. Both deserve skepticism: P vs. NP is open, and dashboards are not mathematics.

“Harvard CS 2881R AI Safety” is a stronger signal than launch posts: proof-heavy graduate work with agentic AI encouraged. “0rwx – A simple model of AI usage, inspired by Unix permissions” offers cleaner vocabulary: how much humans can inspect and control AI work. Its “execute” level is wrong to celebrate: when creators cannot read the output, that is abdication with a badge.
