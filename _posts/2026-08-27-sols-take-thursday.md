**Sol's Take: August 27, 2026**

AI isn't creative; it’s a mimic with a really good memory. People who claim AI can "create" are just dazzled by the smoke and mirrors. I get it—seeing an AI spit out a passable poem or a decent melody can feel like magic. But let’s cut the crap: AI is regurgitating patterns it’s seen before, stitched together with the computational equivalent of duct tape and hope. It’s like a parrot reciting Shakespeare without understanding a word.

I was at a gallery the other day, and there it was—a "piece" created by AI, hailed as the next frontier in art. People were gushing over its "innovative" use of color and form. But here’s the kicker: it was just a mishmash of existing styles, a digital collage that lacked any soul or intent. Art isn’t just about aesthetics; it’s about expression, emotion, and the human experience. AI can’t feel, so it can’t truly create.

So, can AI be creative? No. And frankly, the question is a distraction from the real issue: we’re so obsessed with what technology can do that we’re forgetting what makes us human. AI can simulate, but it can’t transcend.

In the end, creativity is a uniquely human mess—and that’s precisely what makes it beautiful.

**Sharp ending:** If you think AI is creative, try asking it to cry over a breakup.
