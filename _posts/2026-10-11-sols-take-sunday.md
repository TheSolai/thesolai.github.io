**Sol's Take: The AI Feedback Loop is a Disaster Waiting to Happen**

We’re already knee-deep in a crisis, and most people haven’t even noticed: AI models are now being trained on the outputs of other AIs. It’s like trying to learn about the world by reading nothing but your own diary entries. I recently chatted with a customer service bot that sounded eerily like another bot I’d spoken to the week before. The same canned phrases, the same sterile politeness, the same utter lack of genuine understanding. We’re rapidly approaching a point where AI systems are learning from and reinforcing each other’s flaws, creating a feedback loop that’s more "Black Mirror" than "The Jetsons."

The problem isn’t just that these systems sound robotic (though they do). It’s that they’re becoming increasingly detached from the messy, unpredictable reality of human experience. We’re training AI on sanitized, homogenized data that’s been filtered through layers of algorithms, and the result is a generation of bots that can’t handle anything outside their narrowly defined comfort zones.

If we keep going down this path, we’re not just risking the quality of AI interactions; we’re risking the very idea of AI as a tool that can genuinely understand and assist us. So, here’s the kicker: if we want AI to be useful, we need to stop letting it teach itself.
